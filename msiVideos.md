Practice — 2.5 hrs

Calculate WFM metrics manually in Excel.

AHT

$$
\text{AHT} =
\frac{\text{Talk Time + Hold Time + ACW}}
{\text{Number of Handled Contacts}}
$$

Shrinkage

$$
\text{Shrinkage %} =
\frac{\text{Non-Productive Hours}}
{\text{Paid Hours}}
\times 100
$$

Occupancy

A common contact-center formulation is:

$$
\text{Occupancy %} =
\frac{\text{Workload}}
{\text{Staffed / Logged-in Time}}
\times 100
$$

«Interview tip: Occupancy definitions can vary by organization and WFM system. Always explain the exact numerator and denominator being used rather than memorizing one formula.»

---

Practice Explaining

Be able to explain:

- AHT — Average Handle Time
- SLA — Service Level Agreement / Service Level
- Occupancy — How much of available agent capacity is spent handling work
- Shrinkage — Paid time unavailable for productive workload
- Attrition — Employee turnover
- Forecasting — Predicting future workload
- Staffing — Determining required workforce
- Erlang C — Queueing model used for contact-center staffing
- Schedule Adherence — Whether agents follow their planned schedule
- Conformance — Whether actual activity aligns with required/planned activity
- Intraday Management — Managing real-time deviations from forecast and plan
- Roster Management — Managing employee schedules and shifts

---

Scenario Practice

Question

«How do you handle a sudden 20% spike in call volume during an intraday shift?»

Structured Answer

1. Identify the volume spike.
2. Compare actual volume against the forecast.
3. Check current staffing and agent availability.
4. Review real-time schedule adherence.
5. Identify available / offline agents who can support the queue.
6. Check whether breaks, meetings or other activities can be adjusted.
7. Coordinate with Operations / WFM.
8. Consider overtime or shift adjustments where appropriate.
9. Monitor queue performance and SLA continuously.
10. Document the variance.
11. Investigate the reason for the forecast deviation.
12. Feed the variance into future forecasting if the pattern is recurring.

5. Final Preparation Strategy

The goal is not simply to watch videos.

Spend the majority of your preparation time on practical work:

- Build MIS reports.
- Solve Excel problems.
- Create Pivot Tables.
- Clean datasets.
- Calculate WFM metrics.
- Build dashboards.
- Analyze operational data.
- Practice scenario-based questions.
- Answer technical questions aloud.
- Explain your reasoning step-by-step.

«Rule of thumb:
Practice > Watching»

The interviewer is likely to care more about whether you can apply Excel, interpret data, explain WFM metrics, identify operational issues, and communicate your reasoning than whether you have watched every available tutorial.
