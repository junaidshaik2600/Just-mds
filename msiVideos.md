## 2. 3-Day Preparation Schedule

**Total Watching Time:** ~4 Hours 30 Minutes  
**Remaining Time:** Practical hands-on practice in Excel/Google Sheets.

---

### Day 1: Advanced Excel & MIS Core

#### Watch — 1 hr 20 mins

- *Excel Formulas & Functions* (Kevin Stratvert) — **35 mins**
- *MIS Executive Course* (Skills Improvement) — **45 mins**

#### Practice — 2.5 hrs

Open Excel and create sample datasets. Practice writing:

- Nested `IF`
- `XLOOKUP`
- `INDEX-MATCH`
- `SUMIFS`
- `COUNTIFS`
- `TEXTJOIN`

Also practice:

- Creating Pivot Tables

- Basic MIS report creation

### Day 2: WFM Concepts, Metrics & NICE IEX

#### Watch — 1 hr 10 mins

- *Basics on WFM* (Call Center WFM) — **18 mins**
- *Manage Shrinkage & SLA* (WFM Tipsy Thursdays) — **14 mins**
- *NICE WFM Simulation & Engagement* (NICE Official) — **6 mins**
- *MIS & WFM Interview Q&A* (Career with Omkar) — **22 mins**

#### Practice — 2.5 hrs

Calculate key WFM metrics manually in Excel using formulas.

### Shrinkage

$$
\text{Shrinkage \%} =
\frac{\text{Total Non-Productive Hours}}
{\text{Total Paid Hours}}
\times 100
$$

### Occupancy

$$
\text{Occupancy \%} =
\frac{\text{Total Handle Time}}
{\text{Logged-in Time} - \text{Shrinkage}}
\times 100
$$

Practice explaining:

- AHT
- SLA
- Occupancy
- Shrinkage
- Attrition
- Forecasting
- Staffing
- Erlang C
- Schedule adherence
- Conformance
- Intraday management
- Roster management

#### Scenario Practice

Example:

> **How do you handle a sudden 20% spike in call volume during an intraday shift?**

Prepare a structured response covering:

1. Identify the volume spike.
2. Compare actual volume against forecast.
3. Check staffing and availability.
4. Review real-time adherence.
5. Identify available/offline agents.
6. Coordinate with Operations/WFM.
7. Consider overtime, breaks, or shift adjustments where appropriate.
8. Monitor SLA and queue performance.
9. Document the variance and update the forecast if required.

## 4. Priority Order for the 3-Day Preparation

If time becomes limited, focus on the following order:

1. **Advanced Excel / Google Sheets**
2. **MIS reporting and dashboards**
3. **WFM concepts and metrics**
4. **Intraday management and scenario questions**
5. **NICE IEX fundamentals**
6. **Looker Studio**
7. **Power BI**
8. **Tableau**

The goal is not just to watch the videos. Spend the majority of the available preparation time **building reports, solving Excel problems, calculating WFM metrics, and answering scenario-based interview questions aloud**.
